## FAQ Section

### Section Title

**Common Questions.**

### Subheadline

Everything you need to know about the system, technology, and operations.

### Questions & Answers

#### Q1: Is there really no setup fee?

**A:** None. We believe your software should be an investment in growth, not a barrier to entry. You can be live in 15 minutes.

#### Q2: Can I use my existing Devices & hardware?

**A:** Yes. Codeket runs on any tablet (iPad, Android) or browser. No proprietary hardware lock-in or forced expensive terminal upgrades.

#### Q3: How does the offline mode work?

**A:** Our local-first architecture stores all transaction data in your Device's persistent storage. If the internet fails, everything keeps running. Once the connection is restored, it automatically syncs with your core database.

#### Q4: What kind of support do I get?

**A:** Every pilot customer gets direct access to the founding team. You get a direct line for WhatsApp or calls. We don't do ticket waitlists.

#### Q5: How flexible is the tax system?

**A:** Taxes can be configured globally, per location, and per individual menu item. Inclusive or exclusive pricing is supported, making Codeket suitable for international tax environments where rigid POS systems fail.
